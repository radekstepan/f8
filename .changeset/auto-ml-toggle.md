Color Adjustments sections with an automatic action now carry a single header toggle instead of separate Auto/ML and reset buttons. Toggling on applies the suggested change, toggling off resets the section to defaults, and dragging a slider lights the toggle on its own. The model-driven tools (Enhance Faces, Subject & Background) keep a small ML badge by the tool name. The whole-photo Auto Enhance pill, the Repair tool, and straightening are unchanged.
